UAE Company Formation Structures

Select the perfect corporate entity structure tailored to your business activities and ownership requirements.

01

LLC (Limited Liability)

Ideal for commercial trading & local service providers. Allows unrestricted trading across all UAE emirates.

02

Sole Establishment

Owned 100% by a single individual for professional services, consultancies, or crafts.

03

Branch / Representative

Extend an established foreign company or local entity into a new territory with full operational continuity.

04

Free Zone Entity (FZE/FZ-LLC)

Enjoy zero income tax, 100% foreign ownership, and zero customs duties inside designated free zones.
