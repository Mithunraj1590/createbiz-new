UAE Tax Thresholds at a Glance

Know your statutory filing requirements under Federal Decree-Laws.

AED 375,000

Mandatory VAT Threshold

Companies exceeding AED 375k taxable supplies in 12 months must register for VAT within 20 business days.

AED 375,000

Corporate Tax 0% Limit

Taxable net profits up to AED 375,000 are taxed at 0%. Net profits above AED 375k attract 9% tax rate.

AED 3,000,000

Small Business Relief

Eligible resident business entities with revenue below AED 3m can elect Small Business Relief treatment.
