Privacy Policy

How CreateBiz collects, protects, and uses your personal & corporate information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as name, email address, contact telephone number, and company incorporation details when you submit consultation requests or use our online cost calculator.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is strictly utilized to process your business setup requests, facilitate trade licensing approvals with UAE government authorities, and provide requested corporate advisory services.

3. Data Protection & Security

CreateBiz employs bank-grade SSL encryption and secure data handling procedures in compliance with UAE Data Protection Regulations (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

4. Disclosure to Government Authorities

Corporate information and passport identification copies are disclosed only to official UAE licensing bodies (DED, Free Zone Authorities, Ministry of Economy, GDRFA) as required by law for trade license and visa processing.

Your Data Rights & Choices

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal contact data stored on our consulting servers.

  • Right to request copy of stored personal records
  • Right to opt-out of marketing communications
  • Right to request permanent data deletion upon license completion
